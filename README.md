## My Favorite Movie

### Title

**Three Idiots**

### Summary

*Three Idiots* is a 2009 Indian comedy-drama directed by Rajkumar Hirani. The story follows three engineering students, Rancho, Farhan, and Raju, through their chaotic college life at the Imperial College of Engineering. Rancho, the free-thinking rebel of the group, constantly challenges the traditional education system and encourages his friends to pursue passion over pressure. The narrative jumps between their college days and the present, as Farhan and Raju search for their long-lost friend years later. Beneath the humor and chaos lies a sharp critique of academic competition and the societal obsession with success.

### Reflection

This movie hits like a philosophical TED Talk disguised as a meme fest. It taught me that intelligence isn’t about cramming formulas; it’s about curiosity and creativity. Rancho’s mantra, *“All is well,”* isn’t just catchy; it’s a reminder that panic never solved a problem. The film redefined what “success” really means, urging me to chase excellence instead of external validation. It’s funny, heartfelt, and surprisingly radical, proof that a Bollywood movie can roast the education system and still make you cry, laugh, and rethink your life choices.
